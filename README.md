# SKYLANDERS Font
A recreation of the font seen in many Skylanders logos and branding related materials (a modified version of the font VELVET).

Many vector graphics and icons can be used via this font. Please see below on instructions.

n - curved internal edges. As seen in the Giants, Spyro's Universe, Lost Islands, Battlegrounds, and Collection Vault logos

Contextual alternatives:
L, followed by A, a, E, or N - the letters A, a, E, and N, shrink to lie on top of the L. "L A N" design from Skylanders logo. "L a" design seen in the Lost Islands logo
A, a, E, or N, followed by R - one of the letters A, a, E, and N, sits atop an extruded edge from the side of the R. "E R" design seen in the Skylanders logo

Ligatures based from logos:
E N - bottom stroke of E extrudes into M. As seen in the Spyro's Adventure logo
F o R / F o r - top stroke of F extrudes over o, shrinking it and joining with a split into the R/r. As seen in the SWAP Force logo
L o P / L o p - o sits on top of L, which meets with an extrusion from the back of P/p. A nod to the Tealgamemaster Developer Commentary logo

p Y - tail of Y extrudes all the way to the stem of p. As seen in the SuperChargers Christmas Carol "HAPPY HOLIDAYS"
p y - tail of y extrudes slightly toward the stem of p. As seen in the Spyro's Universe
T u - stroke of T extrudes into u. As seen in the Spyro's Adventure logo

## Notes

The kerning between many glyphs is currently not set correctly, and so certain glyphs may move and appear closer to other certain glyphs than intended when typed out next to each other

This document refers to the Spyro's Universe logo instead of the Universe (without "Spyro's") logo. The Universe part of both logos is exactly identical apart from the width and formatting, and so the added text in the original name makes it more advantageous to reference

The A, N, and E that sit above the L and R in the Skylanders logo are shrunken down, thus having different weights than normal - additionally the curves on the A and N have been altered to be more rounded. These aspects are not kept, and instead a consistent weight and design is kept across the typeface

The A and N in the Spyro's Adventure logo actually have the concave curve on their internal edge more rounded. This is not present

There is an alternate/early (?) Battlegrounds logo with lime green text and an alternate L design. This is similar to the alternate L design ligature glyph present, but not exactly the same

The typeface that this font was derrived from, VELVET, isn't just used for the brands. Another heavily modified version of the font is used for the Soul Gems in Giants, SWAP Force, SuperChargers, and Imaginators, an altered version very similar to this one is used for the SWAP Force enemy intros (hence why that is referenced many times for the design of some glyphs), and the basic, unmodified VELVET font itself is also used in the Spyro's Adventure and Trap Team Soul Gems. Due to certain design parallels, it's even possible that the fonts used for Battlecast and Ring of Heroes are both heavily modified versions of what was originally the VELVET typeface, but only because I can't seem to find fonts that are any closer to those logos than this custom Skylanders brand font


## References

The Super Chargers logo mentioned in this document does not refer to the final "Skylanders SuperChargers" logo, as that does not use this font (excluding the "Skylanders" part). The logo in question is actually the prototype logo for SuperChargers before it was finalised, whilst the game still went under the name "Super Chargers". This logo can be spotted on Steve Moss' website and in the developer menu in Skylanders SuperChargers Racing. In addition to a prototype logo, "Super Chargers" was potentially planned to be a series, having its own games potentially under the "Super Chargers" franchise, notably Racing (which would somewhat see the light of day, it's unclear whether this refers to the same game), Crash Kart Derby, and Construction. All 3 of these conceptual games also have conceptual logos, each using the Skylanders font
https://www.moss.media/skylanders-superchargers

SuperChargers Christmas Carol, a YouTube video uploaded to the official YouTube channel, has a logo shown close to the very end of the video (with a frame while it is shown used for the thumbnail of the aforementioned video), which is a modified version of the SuperChargers logo, most notably to say the phrase "HAPPY HOLIDAYS". As it aims to be a modified version of the SuperChargers logo, the "HAPPY" text is what substitutes the "SKYLANDERS" text, resulting in it using the Skylanders font in this graphic. This is one of the very few times (perhaps only time, actually) that the font was not used for a game logo
